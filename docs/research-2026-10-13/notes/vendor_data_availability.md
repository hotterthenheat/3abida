## Q1. Unusual Whales API: what each exposure-related endpoint returns, its timestamps, history, and how UW computes GEX

### Takeaway
UW Basic gives (a) a per-minute **aggregate** (whole-ticker) GEX/vanna/charm time series for any past date within a 730-trading-day window, (b) per-strike and per-strike×expiry exposure only as a **single "latest" snapshot per date** (for past dates, rebuilt at the close), never per-strike by minute, (c) daily OI-based greek exposure by strike/expiry/date, (d) historical per-contract end-of-day greeks/IV/OI/NBBO via `option-chains?date=&greeks=true` and `greeks?date=&expiry=`, (e) per-contract 1-minute trade bars with IV high/low, and (f) a downloadable full trade tape (every print with its IV, delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho, theo, OI, underlying price and NBBO) that is reachable on Basic. Per-strike exposure by minute for a past session can only be reconstructed by the user (e.g., from the full tape + daily OI); UW does not serve it.

**`GET /api/stock/{ticker}/spot-exposures` ("GEX exposures per 1min")** — params `ticker`, `date` (optional, default last trading date)
- "Returns the GEX exposures for the given ticker per minute." "This data updates about once per minute during the cash session." Websocket `gex:{TICKER}` streams the same (not on Basic). — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- Fields (all numeric strings): `gamma_per_one_percent_move_oi` ("gamma * open interest * price * price"), `gamma_per_one_percent_move_vol` ("gamma * volume * price * price"), `gamma_per_one_percent_move_dir` ("based on directionalized volume"), `vanna_per_one_percent_move_oi` ("vanna * open interest"), `vanna_..._vol` ("vanna * volume"), `vanna_..._dir`, `charm_per_one_percent_move_oi` ("charm * open interest * 365"), `charm_..._vol` ("charm * volume * 365"), `charm_..._dir`, `price` ("The underlying price used in calculations. NOTE: For any index ticker this will be the current ATM strike."), `time` ("The UTC timestamp of the calculation"). — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- Live responses also carry `start_time` (the minute bucket), `ticker`, `ticker_id`. SPY 2026-10-09: 527 rows, first `time` 2026-10-09T10:35:23Z (06:35 ET), last 20:00:58Z; SPY 2025-10-14: 534 rows 10:30Z–20:00Z; SPY 2024-10-15: 538 rows 10:30Z–20:00Z. So the per-minute aggregate series is available historically for the full 730-day window, covering roughly 06:30–16:00 ET. — live probe 2026-10-13, [GET /api/stock/SPY/spot-exposures?date=2024-10-15](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/stock/SPY/spot-exposures?date=2024-10-15)
- Works for SPX on Basic: SPX 2026-10-09 returned 521 rows with `price` "7795"/"7810" (an ATM strike, not the index level). — live probe 2026-10-13, [GET /api/stock/SPX/spot-exposures?date=2026-10-09](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/stock/SPX/spot-exposures?date=2026-10-09)
- In the earliest pre-market rows the `_vol` and `_dir` fields are "0" (no volume yet) and on 2025-10-14 the first `_oi` values were orders of magnitude smaller than later in the day (e.g., gamma_oi -28,728 at 10:30Z). — live probe 2026-10-13 (see Inferences).

**`GET /api/stock/{ticker}/spot-exposures/strike` ("GEX exposures by strike")** — params `ticker`, `date`, `min_strike`, `max_strike`, `limit` (≤500), `page`
- "Returns the most recent GEX exposures across all strikes for the given ticker on a given date. Calculated either with open interest or with volume." — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- Fields per strike: `call_/put_` × `gamma|delta|vanna|charm` × `_oi|_vol|_ask|_bid`, plus `strike`, `price`, `time`. "_ask": "exposure when Market Makers are selling contracts when a transaction is closer to the ask side"; "_bid": buying, closer to the bid. "To get the full directionalized exposure, just sum up the call ask, call bid, put ask and put bid of a greek and strike." No vega field. — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- Timestamp semantics (verbatim points): "Each strike carries the `time` and `price` of its last recalculation. A strike is recalculated when one of its contracts gets new greeks or a new trade, or when the daily open interest loads, at most once per second per ticker. A price move alone does not recalculate a strike. Strikes within one response can differ in `time`, possibly by hours ..." "A book computed after the session ... gives every strike the same `time`, no later than 15 minutes after the regular close ... For a past date that is rebuilt from end-of-day data, every strike instead carries the regular session close as `time`, and as `price` the closing price for stocks and ETFs or, for index tickers such as SPX, the last at-the-money price estimate recorded that day." Also: "a strike recalculation with a negative call gamma or a positive put gamma is discarded"; summing strikes gives "approximately" the per-minute ticker totals; "Narrowing with `min_strike` or `max_strike` leaves out the exposure of the excluded strikes"; pages can return 503 while loading (retry after `Retry-After`). — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- There is no time-of-day parameter: the endpoint takes a `date`, never a time. — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi); same conclusion recorded in `/home/user/3abida/docs/data-feed.md`
- Past dates return one close snapshot: SPY 2025-10-14 rows carried `time` 2025-10-14T20:00:00Z, `price` 662.23; SPY 2024-10-15 rows carried `time` 20:14:20Z and several `*_delta_*` fields were `null`. — live probe 2026-10-13, [GET /api/stock/SPY/spot-exposures/strike?date=2024-10-15](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/stock/SPY/spot-exposures/strike?date=2024-10-15&limit=2)

**`GET /api/stock/{ticker}/greek-exposure`** (`date`, `timeframe` e.g. YTD/1D/1W/1M/1Y, default 1Y), **`/greek-exposure/strike`** (`date`), **`/greek-exposure/expiry`** (`date`), **`/greek-exposure/strike-expiry`** (`date`, `expiry`)
- Daily rows: `call_/put_` `gamma|gex`, `delta`, `charm`, `vanna`, each "the sum of the [greek] values of all call transactions that executed multiplied by the open interest and the number of shares per contract (typically 100 shares per contract)"; keyed by `date`, `strike` and/or `expiry`. **How UW computes GEX (from the spec)**
- "GEX is the assumed $ value of the given greek (ie. gamma) exposure that market makers need to hedge per 1% change of the underlying stock's price movement. A positive value is long and a negative value is short." "GEX assumes that market makers are part of every transaction and that the bulk of their transactions are buying calls and selling puts to investors hedging their portfolios." Per contract: "$[0.05 * 100 shares * 0.01 * stock price * underlying parameter of the greek variable (for gamma this variable is the stock price)]"; totals sum "all open contracts determined by the daily open interest or by volume." — [UW OpenAPI spec, spot-exposures](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- Three bases: `_oi` (daily OI), `_vol` (the day's volume), `_dir` / `_ask`+`_bid` ("directionalized volume": trade nearer the ask → MM sold → negative for call gamma; nearer the bid → MM bought). — [UW OpenAPI spec, spot-exposures/strike](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi)
- Charm `_oi`/`_vol` are multiplied by 365 (annualized); the repo divides by 365 so a charm reads per day, after checking the strike rows against gamma live. — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi); `/home/user/3abida/docs/data-feed.md`
- Sign convention: calls positive gamma, puts negative (dealer long calls / short puts); live SPY rows show `call_gamma_oi` > 0 and `put_gamma_oi` < 0. The repo maps them onto its own sign by negating gamma, delta and the calls' vanna and charm. — live probe 2026-10-13; `/home/user/3abida/docs/data-feed.md`
- UW's education post describes net gamma by strike as "a naive calculation meant to show you where we've built up open interest by strike". — [UW Substack, "How to understand Gamma, Delta, GEX, DEX"](https://unusualwhales.substack.com/p/how-to-understand-gamma-delta-gex)

### Inferences
- **Per-strike exposure by minute for a past date is not served** on any UW endpoint: by-strike and by-expiry-strike take only `date` and return one snapshot (the close, for rebuilt past dates). Reconstructing a strike-by-minute surface requires recomputing from inputs: daily OI per contract (option-chains `date`/oi-per-strike), per-trade IV/greeks/underlying price (full tape), or per-contract minute IV high/low (intraday) — inferred from the parameter lists above.
- The per-minute aggregate (`spot-exposures`) is the only intraday-by-time exposure series. It can serve as a check-sum for a reconstruction ("Summing every strike ... gives approximately the ticker level values") — but only for totals, not a strike profile.
- The tiny early `_oi` values on 2025-10-14 at 06:30 ET suggest the minute series can predate that day's OI load (UW says OI change lands ~06:45 ET). Inferred; treat pre-07:00 ET rows with care.
- The "1-minute SPX Market Maker Exposure" that Basic lacks appears to be a separate Periscope-type product, not the `spot-exposures` endpoint, because `spot-exposures` for SPX answered on Basic. Inferred — no UW page defines the item.
- The full tape is probably the richest reconstruction input on Basic (point-in-time IV/greeks/OI/underlying per print), but at ~1–2 GB zipped per day it is a bulk-download job, not an API call per minute.
- `greek-exposure` (daily) and `spot-exposures` are in different units; they should not be mixed without rescaling.

### Gaps
- UW publishes no plan-by-endpoint matrix; whether any of these endpoints is Advanced-only could only be checked by live calls (all of the above answered on Basic).
- The exact UW greeks model (pricing model, rate, dividend, IV source) is not documented in the spec.
- Why `option-contract/{id}/historic` for SPY260116C00700000 started at 2025-01-02 (inside the 730-day window but after its likely listing date) is unexplained.
- Whether full-tape `open_interest` per print is that morning's OCC figure (prior close) was not verified row by row.
- UW's help-center GEX articles could not be located by search; only the spec and the Substack post were found.

## Q2. Massive (formerly Polygon.io) Basic: what options, stock and index inputs exist, at what recency and depth

### Takeaway
On Massive Basic (free, 5 calls/min, end-of-day, 2 years) the only options-exposure inputs are **per-contract minute/day OHLCV aggregates** (trade-derived, 2 years, end-of-day) and the **contract reference list with `as_of`/`expired`**. Snapshots (greeks, IV, open interest), trades, quotes and flat files are all excluded, and there is no historical open-interest or greeks endpoint on any plan's REST list. Stocks Basic gives 2 years of minute bars (end-of-day); Indices Basic is "Limited Index Tickers", and `I:SPX` answered 403 on this key.

### Cited Findings

## Q3. OCC/Cboe: when open interest is computed and published, how vendors date it, SPX vs SPXW, AM vs PM settlement

### Takeaway
Open interest is computed by OCC overnight from the day's trade reports (opening/closing designations) and exercises/assignments, shown the next morning, and is static intraday. UW stamps a contract's OI with the **next** trading date: the row dated D holds the OI as of the close of D−1, verified on newly listed contracts (OI 0 on the first trading day). SPX (root SPX) is AM-settled on the third Friday and stops trading the business day before; SPXW (weeklys, EOM, dailies) is PM-settled and trades until 16:00 ET on expiration day (13:00 on half days).

### Cited Findings
- "The OCC is responsible for calculating open interest. This process involves consolidating trade reports from the exchanges, which detail all opening and closing transactions for the day. In addition, the OCC accounts for options that were exercised and resulted in assignment, since those contracts are 'closed' and eliminated from open interest." — [OIC (OCC's education arm), "Open Interest: Why It Matters", Oct 2025](https://www.optionseducation.org/news/open-interest-why-it-matters)
- Open interest is "a lagging number: it's not updated during the course of a trading day", "officially posted by The OCC the morning after any given trading session", and "for the rest of the trading day the figure remains static". — [Options Playbook](https://optionsplaybook.com/options-introduction/open-interest)
- "Open interest is calculated centrally each end-of-day by the Options Clearing Corp (OCC) ... The following morning, open interest is displayed on the OCC web site" but "is not distributed industry-wide until the next business day morning"; at Alpaca "the open interest data for day 1 will always lag and be available on day 3", so check `open_interest_date`. — [Alpaca forum](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/old-option-open-interest-dates/18346) (vendor-specific; contrasts with UW below)
- OCC publishes a downloadable Daily Open Interest report by date (`https://marketdata.theocc.com/daily-open-interest?reportDate=MM/DD/YYYY&action=download&format=csv`, per the search snippet; OCC's page returned 403 to the fetcher, so the page itself was not read). — [OCC Daily Open Interest (search result)](https://www.theocc.com/market-data/market-data-reports/other-market-data-info/batch-processing/daily-open-interest)
- UW timing: market OI change "updates once on trading days at around 6:45am EST in the premarket"; the pricing page lists "Daily Open Interest including FLEX OI transfer"; `option-contracts.prev_oi` is "The previous trading day's open interest". — [UW OpenAPI spec](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/openapi); [UW pricing](https://unusualwhales.com/pricing?product=api)
- UW dating, verified: SPY261120C00800000 historic rows: 2026-05-12 volume 39, OI 0; 2026-05-13 OI 38; SPY261030C00790000: 2026-04-27 volume 5, OI 0; 2026-04-28 OI 5; 2026-04-29 OI 7 (5+2); 2026-04-30 volume 186, OI 7; 2026-05-01 OI 191. The OI on the row dated D therefore reflects trading through D−1 (OCC's overnight count after D−1's close). — live probe 2026-10-13, [GET /api/option-contract/SPY261030C00790000/historic](https://api.unusualwhales.com/api/option-contract/SPY261030C00790000/historic). The repo uses the same rule: "a count of open interest dated D is the close of the session before D". — `/home/user/3abida/docs/data-feed.md`
- Massive dates OI to "the end of the last trading day" in its snapshot. — [Massive docs, Option Chain Snapshot](https://massive.com/docs/rest/options/snapshots/option-chain-snapshot.md)
- Cboe S&P 500 option products: SPX, root "SPX", "AM" settlement, "3rd Friday", cash; SPXW (Weeklys/EOM), root "SPXW", "PM", "Mon., Tue., Wed., Th., Fri., 3rd Fri. and Last Trading Day of Month", cash; XSP PM; SPY PM, physical. "In the case of a holiday on the settlement date, the settlement date is moved back one business day" (Monday Weeklys move forward). — [Cboe SPX Weeklys specifications](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/sp_500/spx_weekly_options/specifications/)
- "Trading in SPX options will ordinarily cease on the business day (usually a Thursday) preceding the day on which the exercise-settlement value (i.e., the expiration date) is calculated, 5:00 pm ET. Trading in SPXW options will ordinarily cease on the day of expiration, 4:00 pm ET, and at 1:00 pm ET for any half day holiday." Regular hours 9:30–16:15 ET; curb 16:15–17:00; Global Trading Hours 20:15–09:25 ET. — [Cboe SPX options specifications](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/sp_500/spx_options/specifications/)
- AM-settled SPX settles on the Special Opening Quotation (SOQ) of the S&P 500. — [Federal Register 2012 (Cboe SPXPM filing)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2012-07-26/pdf/2012-18243.pdf) (via search summary; the PDF was not opened)
- UW keeps SPX and SPXW as separate roots in its listings (SPX/SPXW, NDX/NDXP, RUT/RUTW, VIX/VIXW AM). — `/home/user/3abida/docs/data-feed.md` (from UW's expiry-breakdown)

### Inferences
- For a historical reconstruction at minute m of session D, the point-in-time OI is UW's OI dated D (prior close). Using OI dated D+1 would leak D's own opening/closing trades.
- On SPX monthly expiration Friday, the AM-settled SPX contracts have stopped trading (Thursday) and settle at the open, so they should drop out of any Friday-intraday exposure book; same-day SPXW contracts stay in until 16:00 ET.
- OCC counts include exercises/assignments, so the morning-after OI for an expiring series is not its last intraday position (inferred from the OIC description).

### Gaps
- OCC's own pages (daily OI report, publication time) returned 403; the exact OCC publication hour was not confirmed from a primary OCC page.
- How UW and Massive treat FLEX OI transfers in strike totals was not documented beyond the field `flex_oi_transfer`.
- Cboe's half-day and holiday calendar for SPXW expirations was not fetched.

### Gaps

- Storage and download cost of the full tape at 730 days (~290–450 GB per year of Parquet per UW's own estimate) was not measured here.
