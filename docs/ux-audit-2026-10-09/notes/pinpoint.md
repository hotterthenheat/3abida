# Pinpoint room — UX/UI audit (/pinpoint and every sub-page, plus the legacy routes)

Method, so the report writer can weigh the evidence: the running dev app at http://localhost:5300 (Vite dev server, so the timings run slower than a production build would), driven by Playwright/Chromium with scripted clicks, menu opens, keyboard walks, DOM measurements (font sizes, WCAG contrast, overflow and truncation, target sizes, accessible names) and screenshots. Viewports: 1440×900, 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 390×844, dark and light (`slayer_theme`). Timezones: the container's own (UTC), plus `America/New_York` and `Europe/London`. Source read: `src/App.tsx`, `src/pages/pinpoint/*`, and the `src/components/gex/*` widgets the pages mount. All screenshots are in `shots-pinpoint/` next to this file. The data is simulated by design and is not flagged. Only visible words, labels and contradictions between pages are flagged.

Severity scale: **Broken** (does not work / wrong destination), **Major** (misleads a trader or blocks a task), **Minor** (friction, inconsistency), **Polish**.

Console: no errors, page errors or failed requests on any Pinpoint page, viewport or theme. The only messages were the two React Router "v7 future flag" warnings, logged once per load on every route ([sweep output in scratchpad]; e.g. `shots-pinpoint/redirect_pinpoint-gex_targets.png`). No visible banned word (grade, score, win rate, signal, guaranteed, confluence, market intelligence, simulated, demo, fake, preview, pretend, at launch) appeared in any page text, title, aria-label or placeholder at any viewport.

## 1. Does every control on every sub-page work, give feedback, and keep the active symbol?

### Takeaway
Nearly every control works: the dropdowns, the scope chips (follow/unlink), Replay, Names (up to 4), The read, How to read, fullscreen, the alerts, the Board's filter, add and remove, the sorts, the wall picker, the vol scenario, swap, and the row picks that set the shared "Focus" strike. The symbol carries across sub-pages, and so do the per-box "own name" choices. The real problems are: **Targets' "Chart" buttons open the Map's number grid, not a chart**; the Map's "How to read" guide describes views and dates that are not there; several click targets are dead to the keyboard; and a few controls change more than the reader expects.

What: the button is labelled "Chart", but its tooltip says "See this strike on the Map" and it navigates to `/pinpoint/map`. Since 2026-09-12 the Map has no chart (`MapDesk.tsx` header: "The day-on-one-chart box … is GONE"). The stale code comment "THE CHART is the Map's" (RankedTargets.tsx:184) is the root cause. Meanwhile the Focus chip's own "Chart ↗" in the page head goes to `/pulse`, a real chart (`PinpointLayout.tsx:153`). So two "Chart" buttons on one screen go to two different places. Evidence: scripted click → `http://localhost:5300/pinpoint/map` with the Focus chip "474"; the Focus chip's Chart → `http://localhost:5300/pulse` (`shots-pinpoint/targets-ix-after-chart.png`, `shots-pinpoint/targets-ix-focuschip-chart.png`). Fix: point the row and card button at `/pulse` with `{ focusPrice, ticker, from }`, as the Focus chip does, or rename it "Map" with a map icon.
- **Major: the Map's "How to read" guide is for another view.** Where: /pinpoint/map → "How to read" (`ExposureField.tsx:1067` title "How to read the calendar"; body `LedgerGuide.tsx:59-62, 212`). ## 2. Errors, loading, clipping, glitches, small text, contrast — across 4 viewports and both themes

### Takeaway
No console errors or failed requests. The weak spots are: **slow first paint of the Map** (dev build), **a sticky-header bleed in the Matrix**, **overlapping labels in the Trader's clock on a phone**, **cut-off cells on the phone's Compare table**, a **Board too wide for every desktop width**, and **very small type**: 45–76% of text elements on five pages are under 11 px.

### Cited Findings
- **Major (perf, dev build): the Map takes 10–60 s to stand up on a cold load and keeps the main thread busy while idle.** Measurements: cold load to no skeleton 22.3 s, a warm full reload 36.3 s, later runs 8–11 s; 72 long tasks totalling 17.4 s during the cold load (longest 1.56 s). **Idle on the Map for 20 s: 53 long tasks, 8.0 s of main-thread time, longest 703 ms.** In-app navigation: Building 11.1 s, Compare 12.4 s, Targets 5.1 s until their skeletons cleared. On later warm runs the other pages settled in 4–7 s. Evidence: script `perf` output (scratchpad `audit-pinpoint/perf.mjs`); skeleton-only frame `shots-pinpoint/map-dark-1440x900.png` captured 20 s after load. Caveat: Vite dev with React development checks, so production will be faster. The idle cost is still a real signal. Fix: profile a production build; check the per-tick rebuilds in ExposureField / TraderClock / WallReportCard, the 10 s scan cadence and the four on-screen boxes all re-rendering on `marketData`.
- **Minor (glitch): Matrix header bleed.** Where: /pinpoint/map ladder (and every Names column) (`ExposureMatrix.tsx:278`, `thead sticky top-0 bg-panel`). What: between the group row ("GEX · 1% move") and the "PUT CALL NET" row, the scrolled-under row shows through as a strip of half-cut figures ("$4.1B -$6.1B …"), and the first visible strike row (490/491) is cut by the header. It shows in both themes and at every desktop width. Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/map-dark-1440x900-top.png` (crop), `map-light-1440x900-top.png`, `map-ix-names-qqq.png`. Fix: give both header rows an opaque background with no gap (or one `<tr>` with a two-line cell), and set `scroll-padding-top` to the header's height.
- **Major (phone): the Trader's clock phase labels overlap.** Where: /pinpoint/map at 390×844. What: "The open" over "The morning", "Charm window" over "The turn", and the axis "09:30" over "10:00". The five-minute blocks are 4 px wide touch targets. Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/map-dark-390x844-full.png` (crop at y≈2330–3000). Fix: below about 600 px, show only the current and next phase names (the "Now / Next" line already says this) and make the strip one slider.
- **Major (phone): the Compare head-to-head table cuts its values mid-word.** Where: /pinpoint/compare at 390×844. What: "0.21% ov", "+0.73% · $12", "17% of the tir", "of the hedging"; "Expected move to …". Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/compare-dark-390x844-full.png` (y≈1100–1900). - **Major (layout): the Board never fits its columns.** At 1440 with the rail open the grid shows up to "Net GEX t…", and Expires today, Flip crossed today, Today so far and Your positions need a 16 px horizontal scrollbar. At 1920, "Your positions" is still off-screen. On a phone, 2½ columns show. Evidence: header geometry "Net GEX today@1347-1467 … Flip crossed today@1607-1757" against a 1416 px box; `shots-pinpoint/board-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `board-dark-1920x1080-top.png`, `board-dark-390x844-top.png`. This contradicts task #45 "Pinpoint Board (names): fit the info on the screen". Fix: fold "Today so far" and "Your positions" into the Name or Price cell, merge "Supreme" into "Nearest wall", or let the reader hide columns.
- **Major (phone): the page head takes 65% of the first screen.** Where: every Pinpoint page at 390×844. What: icon, name, cut subtitle, four facts in 2×2, a Ruler card and a rule take about 560 of 844 px before any content. Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/map-dark-390x844-top.png`, `board-dark-390x844-top.png`. - **Minor: the page subtitles are cut by `truncate`.** Building at 1440 ("…and where th…"), Map at 1280 ("…how the level…"), all on a phone (`PinpointLayout.tsx:166`). Also "Where it closes" ("the three likeliest numb…"), the ruler subtitle on Compare ("pushes along · dashe…"), and AMD's nearest wall "+0.43%…" on the Board. Evidence: truncation probe, `shots-pinpoint/building-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `ahead-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `compare-dark-1440x900-full.png`. Fix: allow two lines, or shorten the copy.
- **Minor: the Map's box is a fixed 870 px tall.** On 1280×720 the ladder (internal scroll) is taller than the window, so the page and the ladder scroll inside each other, and the CHARM column and the spot "SPY" label are cut at the right edge. - **Works:** no horizontal page scroll at any viewport (docW = viewport); the footer art renders under every page (`shots-pinpoint/wall-dark-1440x900-bottom-footer.png`); both themes render every page with no dark islands left over; and the redirects resolved within about 1 s, warm.

### Inferences
- The perf and small-type problems are the most likely reasons the owner keeps calling pages "unreadable" or slow. Both are systemic: shared head, shared tracked-caps label component, shared scan loops.
- The 870 px Map box, the fixed Board columns and the fixed-width row text all assume a 1440+ screen with the rail open. 1280 and phone layouts are second-class.

### Gaps
- Timings come from the dev server only. No production build was profiled, to keep the repo untouched.
- Contrast was measured on DOM text only. Text drawn in SVG or canvas (chart axes, Building's dots, the Compare ruler) was judged by eye.
- The 1280 and 1920 views were checked in dark only. Light was covered at 1440 and 390.

## 3. Are the sub-pages one coherent room? (naming, layouts, purpose, numbers, colour, tooltips)

### Takeaway
The shell is consistent (one head, the same facts, the same Ruler, the same scope chip on every box, the same "How to read" door) and the copy is plain English. But **the pages disagree with each other about the same numbers**: the call wall is 480 on one page and 481 on the next, reach and hold odds for the same strike differ, and "weakest wall" names different strikes. The clock is in New York on some lines and in the browser's zone on others, and "Since the open" starts before the open. **The Board's Net GEX column contradicts its own tooltip and the Hedging column beside it.**

### Cited Findings
- **Major: the call wall differs between pages, and within one page.** Within about 1 minute: Map (all expiries, Today→Nov 6) and Board → **call wall 480**; Ahead, Building, At the wall, Compare (0DTE, ±30) → **481**; Targets (±15 window) list tags **480** "CALL WALL", while the Targets Alerts menu on the same page says "Price reaches the call wall **481**" (it uses a ±20 window, `RankedTargets.tsx:197` vs `windowMemory = 15`). Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/map-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `ahead-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `wall-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `targets-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `targets-ix-watch-menu.png` text. Fix: compute the named levels (call wall, put wall, flip, supreme) once per name, from one fixed book, in a shared hook, and use the page's window only for which rows to draw. If the Map's expiry choice must change the walls, label them "call wall (Today→Nov 6)".
- **Major: the same strike has different odds on different pages.** 476 on Targets: "reached 46%, holds 74%"; on the Board: "51% reached · holds 77%"; on Compare: "51% reached · holds 75%". 477 on Targets: "reached 73% · holds 70%"; on At the wall: "reached 79%, holds 70%". The call wall's record on the Map is "Tests 40, held 30, broke 10" (480), while At the wall shows "21× · held 2 · broke 19" (481). Evidence: the same screenshots as above. Fix: one shared agenda/wall model per name per scan (the code already says "the Pulse Targets panel reads the same one", `RankedTargets.tsx:24-26`, so extend that to Board and Compare).
- **Major: the Board's Net GEX colours and tooltip contradict each other and the Hedging column.** Where: /pinpoint/board "Net GEX today" (`Board.tsx:186-189`, header tooltip `Board.tsx:338`). What: the header tooltip says "positive absorbs moves, negative amplifies them". The cell paints positive values red, with the title "more puts than calls — dealer hedging makes moves bigger", and the codebase's own convention agrees with the cell (`data/exposure.ts:241-242`: positive → "put gamma dominates and hedging amplifies moves"). On the same row, QQQ and MSFT read "absorbing moves" (green) under Hedging today, next to a red +$277.7M / +$35.1M Net GEX. Evidence: tooltip text captured by hover; `shots-pinpoint/board-dark-1440x900-full.png`, `board-ix-netgex-tooltip.png`. Fix: correct the header tooltip, and either explain that Hedging is "relative to the flip" while Net is "the whole book", or drop one of the two columns. A sign convention where positive Net GEX means amplifying is the opposite of the industry's usual reading, so it needs a stated definition in the guide.
- **Major: "Since the open" and "today" do not start at the open, and the times are in two zones.** In New York's zone, the Map report reads "390 one-minute bars from **07:55** to 14:24", and Compare's "Since the open" axis runs **08:00 → 14:20**, while the Trader's clock on the same page says "New York · 09:30 to 16:00". In UTC the same labels read "11:28 to 18:05" and the axis "11:31 … 18:00", while the head says "To the close 2h 02m" and the clock says "Now 13:58 · lunch". The "updated HH:MM:SS" stamps (Building, At the wall, Targets, Board, Compare) also use the browser's zone. Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/compare-tapes-tz-America-New_York.png`, `compare-dark-1440x900-full.png`; tz script output; code `WallReportCard.tsx:49`, `data/levelview.ts:40` (`toLocaleTimeString` with no `timeZone`), `hhmmss()` in each page. Fix: format every session time with `timeZone: 'America/New_York'` and label it "ET". Anchor "today's bars" at 09:30 ET, or title them "the last 390 minutes".
- **Minor: "weakest wall" names a different strike on each page.** Targets head: "Weakest wall in reach 480 · holds 16%". At the wall: "481 CALL WALL WEAKEST" (18% reach), and minutes later "477 SHELF WEAKEST" (69%) once 481's reach fell to 12%, so the badge jumps. Evidence: `shots-pinpoint/wall-dark-1440x900-full.png` vs `wall-dark-1440x900-bottom-footer.png`. Fix: show the reach threshold in the tooltip ("in reach = over 15% by the close"), and use the same model on both pages.
- **Minor: the strike-window control has two names and two defaults.** Map: "Strikes: 20 each side" (15/20/25/30). Targets: "15 each side" (10/15/20/30). Building: "±15" (±10/15/20/30). Compare: "Reach: Two expected moves". At the wall: none, yet its box is titled "Every wall on the strikes shown" (`WallBoard.tsx:71`). Evidence: menus captured in scripts `map2`, `tgt`, `misc`, `cmp`. Fix: one label ("Strikes · 15 each side"), one option set, one remembered default for the room. Retitle At the wall's box "Every wall in reach".

Positions left the Map on 2026-09-14 (`MapDesk.tsx:330-332`), and the first box is the Matrix, not "the ladder". Evidence: every Map screenshot. ## 4. Accessibility (keyboard, focus rings, labels, 44 px targets on phone)

### Takeaway
The menus are proper Radix menus (arrows work, Esc returns focus to the trigger) and the scope chips carry clear aria-labels. But the focus ring is a faint 1 px, several icon buttons have no accessible name, the rows are mouse-only (see §1), and on a phone **every** control on every Pinpoint page is under 44 px.

### Cited Findings
- **Major: no control on any Pinpoint page meets the 44 px touch rule on a phone.** Measured at 390×844: 22/22 (Map, before load), 46/46 (Ahead), 37/37 (Building), 30/30 (At the wall), 94/94 (Targets), 34/34 (Board), 48/48 (Compare) controls under 44 px in one dimension. Typical sizes: scope chip 76×22 and link 25×22, Chart/Alert 66×22 / 64×22, Colours swatch 16×20, "Keep this strike" 20×18, Trader's clock blocks 12×22 (desktop) and about 4 px wide on a phone, the Board's × 20×20. Evidence: sweep JSON `smallN/ctrlN` per page. The landing has a 44 px rule ("ON A PHONE NOTHING TAKES LESS THAN A FINGER"), and the terminal should match it. Fix: use the landing's `before:` hit-area technique on chips, row actions and icon buttons below `md`.
- **Minor: the focus ring is 1 px at 55% silver on dark** (`outline solid 1px rgba(199,211,232,0.55)`) on every trigger, and the "Colours" swatch button shows **no ring at all** (`outline none`). Evidence: script `kb` output; `shots-pinpoint/map-kb-focus.png`. Fix: 2 px for the terminal's controls, as the landing bar uses, and restore the ring on the swatch.
- **Minor: buttons with no accessible name.** The Map's fullscreen button has only `title="Fullscreen"` and an empty name, so the keyboard walk printed a blank name (`ExposureField.tsx:745`). Compare's fullscreen has a name. Fix: `aria-label`.
- **Minor: accessible names that run together.** Ahead's close-odds rows are named "4831.2%", "479#215%", "475supreme11%" (strike, rank and percent concatenated), which a screen reader speaks as one number. The Map report card says "Call wall481broke 19 of 60" and the Supreme chip "Supreme475 · Oct 9 · $363.5M". Evidence: `ctrls()` listing (script `misc`, `map1`). Fix: `aria-label="483 — 1.2% chance the close lands here"`.
- **Minor: 78 tab stops in the Trader's clock** (one button per five minutes), plus 6 phase buttons, between the Map's ladder and the report card. Evidence: `map1` listing. Fix: one `role="slider"` with arrow keys, or a roving tabindex.
- **Works:** dropdown menus open with Enter and move with the arrows (ArrowDown landed on "Calendar"); Esc closes and returns focus to the trigger (verified on 5 Map menus); the scope chip and link have descriptive labels ("Follows the terminal's SPY — click to give this panel its own name"); the Board filter has an aria-label; the Board's × has `aria-label="Take COIN off the board"`; and the svg charts carry `role="img"` with an aria-label (`TargetsAxis.tsx:88`).

### Inferences
- The keyboard gaps cluster where rows are custom `div` grids. Making those rows buttons fixes most of §1's keyboard findings and this section's at once.

### Gaps
- No screen-reader run (NVDA/VoiceOver). The findings come from accessible-name and role inspection only.
- Reduced-motion behaviour was not tested on Pinpoint.
