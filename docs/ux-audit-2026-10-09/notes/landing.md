# Slayer landing page ("/") — full UX/UI audit

Method, 2026-10-09: the running app at http://localhost:5300/ (Vite dev server, commit db27023), driven by Playwright with headless Chromium 141. Screens checked: 1440×900, 1280×720, 1920×1080, 3440×1440, 768×1024 and 390×844, dark and light, plus `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` at 1440×900 (both themes). Every door was pressed. The scroll was stopped and held 1.5–1.6 s at each stop. The page was tabbed through end to end. Text sizes and contrast were measured from computed styles, and phone tap areas by hit-testing. Screenshots are in `shots-landing/` next to this file. Scripts: scratchpad `audit-landing/` (`measure.mjs`, `sweep.mjs`, `controls.mjs`, `keys.mjs`, `keys2.mjs`, `stage.mjs`, `rm.mjs`, `tap.mjs`, `perf.mjs`, `routes.mjs`). Severity scale: **Broken** (does not work), **Major** (works but badly hurts use or breaks an owner rule), **Minor** (visible flaw), **Polish** (refinement).

## Does every control work, and where does each go?

### Takeaway
Every door goes where it says. None of the 63 links and buttons on the page leads to a 404 or a not-found page. There are three real faults: (a) after Back, a plan door opens the sign-up form scrolled to the bottom; (b) keyboard focus walks through the wall's invisible doors while the tour is on screen, so the tour's rail, rows and doors are not reached by tabbing down the page; (c) the bar's hover highlight lands on the wrong link after a jump.

## Are there console errors, failed requests, blank windows, broken films or stills, layout shifts, overlaps, clipping, horizontal scroll or janky stages?

### Takeaway
The page is technically clean: no errors, no failed requests, no horizontal scroll and almost no layout shift at any size. The faults are visual states: a blank session window under reduced motion, a tour caught half-built at a held stop, blown-up crops on a tablet, and stranded or peeking fragments as the stages hand over.

### Cited Findings

The only console lines are two React Router v7 "future flag" warnings, plus framer-motion's "You have Reduced Motion enabled" under reduced motion. No horizontal overflow at any scroll stop (`scrollWidth == clientWidth`). CLS after a full scroll: 0.0138 max (1440 dark), 0 at 390 light. The scratchpad `landing-health.mjs` found no empty window at any size. — [measure.json](../../../tmp/claude-0/-home-user-3abida/3f13cba1-d81b-5e53-bbee-8bc889ed9a96/scratchpad/audit-landing/measure.json)

### Inferences
- The held-stop faults appear when the main thread is busy (several pages at once, or the light theme's heavier stills decoding). A real visitor on a mid-range laptop with other tabs open is closer to the "under load" runs than to the clean runs. The timer-driven steps need a wall-clock safety net.
- The reduced-motion desk is the only path where the session has no picture at all. It is the one most likely missed in QA, because the phone path (BeatPicture) and the motion path (canvas) both work.

### Gaps
- **The films were not seen.** Playwright's Chromium cannot play H.264 (`canPlayType('video/mp4; codecs="avc1.640028"')` returned ""), so every window fell back to its WebP still. That shows the fallback works, but loop seams, film sharpness and film load timing are unverified. Re-run in Chrome or Edge.
- Scroll smoothness: the frame-time numbers from headless software rendering (p50 50 ms, p95 317 ms while scripted-scrolling 40 px per 16 ms) are not trustworthy without a GPU. Real jank is unmeasured.

## Typography and readability: smallest text, contrast, hierarchy

### Takeaway
The page's own words pass. The smallest live text is 11 px (the footer status line), and every measured body or muted line clears 4.5:1 in both themes. The product inside the windows does not: at 1440 the windows shrink the 1440 px desk to about 784 px, so the terminal's 8–11 px text renders at about 4–6 px. That breaks the owner's 2026-10-06 rule ("smallest product text inside any window is at least 11 px at 1440 wide"), which the 2026-10-07 "whole pages" change reversed.

### Cited Findings
- Live text sizes on the 1440 desk (computed): 11 px ×3 (the footer signature "slayer:~ $ ● live · v2026.10"), 11.5 px (window bars "slayer:~ $ open pulse", "SPY · 09:32"), 12 px ×15, 13–13.5 px, and the main body at 15 px (×33). Heads: 30 / 34 / 40 / 46 / 60 px, the closing 92 px, the quote 121 px (136 px at 1920). At 390: smallest 11 px, body 15 px, quote 38.4 px. At 3440 everything scales by 1.258 (root 20.13 px). — measure.json `sizes`
- **Major, product text inside windows.** The tour and session windows are about 783 px wide at 1440 (screenshot: x 484→1267) and show the whole 1440 px desk, a 0.54 scale. The terminal's 8 px labels render at about 4.4 px, its 11 px rows at about 6 px. Only the session's call chip ("Call wall 475") is legible. — [d1440-02-y900.png](shots-landing/d1440-02-y900.png), [d1440-07-y3150.png](shots-landing/d1440-07-y3150.png), [k-tour-tab.png](shots-landing/k-tour-tab.png). The owner's rule of 2026-10-06 asks for ≥11 px, and the 2026-10-07 note chose whole pages ("website still looks huge"). The two directives conflict and the page currently follows the later one. Fix options: (a) the panel films already exist in the pipeline (`panels.mjs`, TerminalWindow `panel`); use them only for the session and the tour's held state, not as the landing's default; (b) add one readable callout per room (like the session chip) naming what the reader should look at; (c) offer a click-to-enlarge on a window.
- Contrast (WCAG ratio, text over its effective background, opacity applied). Dark: muted text rgb(125,125,125) on rgb(5,5,5) = 4.95:1; window-bar muted on rgb(10,10,10) = 4.81:1; the footer signature's "live" = 13.5:1. Light: muted rgb(64,67,74) on paper rgb(241,240,236) = 8.69:1; "live" rgb(58,79,122) = 7.14:1. No live text element measured under 4.5:1. (The only flag was "Launch terminal", a false positive: its background is the foil gradient image.) — measure.json `lowContrast`, `smallest`
- The owner's own rules are kept: quiet text recedes by tier, not opacity; the closing "Seen enough? Step inside." stands faint at 14 % ink by design (LitLines, Landing.tsx:478) and lights with the scroll; the FAQ, plans and trust copy use textSecondary/textMuted tiers only. — Landing.tsx:836–886, 911–951
- Hierarchy at 1440: the quote at 121 px, then section heads at 46–60 px, room names at 40 px, prices at 44 px, and body at 15 px. The scale is clear and consistent. "A read, never an instruction." (60 px, centred) is the largest mid-page head and outranks the rooms' 50 px head, though the rooms are the product. — [d1440-10-y4500.png](shots-landing/d1440-10-y4500.png). Polish: consider the rooms' head at least equal to the trust head.

The landing's rule is "no tracked caps anywhere on the landing", but the footer is explicitly exempt ("never touched here"). Noted for the owner to decide.

### Inferences
- The page's text layer is in good health. Readability problems come from the product pictures, not the copy. Any fix should target the windows, not the type scale.

### Gaps

## Performance feel and page length

### Takeaway
On a desk the quote paints at about 0.65 s, and the hero is fully composed (words risen, doors in) by about 1.6 s. Length meets the owner's targets at the sizes they measured (1440 desk 8,427 px < 8,500; phone 9,804 px < 10,000). It does not at 1920×1080 (9,389 px), and the tablet sits at 9,996 px. Numbers are from the Vite dev server (unbundled modules, about 6 MB of JS over 148 requests), so absolute load times overstate production.

### Cited Findings
- First paint and largest paint, dev server, no throttling, 1440: FCP 644 ms, LCP 1,964 ms (the h1), load 1,480 ms, 148 requests, about 7.9 MB transferred. Same with 4G-like network (150 ms RTT) and 4× CPU: FCP 944 ms, LCP 7,788 ms. Phone 390, same throttle: FCP 996 ms, LCP 7,932 ms. — perf.mjs output in this audit
- The hero's words are hidden until their animation: each quote word rises and focuses over 0.9 s with a 55 ms stagger (index.css:1754–1757), so the last word settles about 1.35 s after paint. The doors rise at a 700 ms delay plus 0.9 s (`landing-rise [--rise-delay:700ms]`, Landing.tsx:752), so they are not fully there until about 1.6 s. LCP is the h1 because of this animation. — index.css:1641–1643, 1754–1757. Polish fix: start the doors with the tease (about 420 ms), shorten the word rise to about 0.6 s, or leave the h1 at full opacity and animate only the transform and blur, so LCP fires at first paint.
- Layout stability: CLS 0 at load on every run, ≤0.0138 after a full scroll. — measure.json
- Page length (scrollHeight): 1440×900 8,427 · 1280×720 7,460 · 1920×1080 **9,389** · 3440×1440 12,245 (scaled ×1.258) · 768×1024 **9,996** · 390×844 9,804 · reduced motion 1440 7,748. The owner's targets: <8,500 desktop and <10,000 phone. — measure.json, landing-health.mjs. **Minor:** the stage tracks are in svh, so a taller screen makes a longer page; 1920×1080 runs 889 px over the desktop target, and the tablet is 4 px under the phone ceiling. Fix: cap the svh-based tracks (TRACK, PROLOGUE, SEG) with `min(…svh, …rem)`.
- Holding on a scroll stop: see the second question. The stages hold in isolation, and under load they can stop mid-way.
- Transfer: the films and stills load lazily (TerminalWindow `lazy`), and the other theme's stills load only when the reader reaches for the theme button (TerminalWindow `warmOtherGround`, verified in code at Landing.tsx:655–656). — Landing.tsx, TerminalWindow.tsx
- **Polish, console noise:** two React Router v7 future-flag warnings on every load, and framer-motion's reduced-motion warning. Dev-only, but they hide real warnings. Fix: opt into `v7_startTransition` and `v7_relativeSplatPath` in the router, and pass `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.

### Inferences
- In production (bundled, the landing in the first chunk at about 195 KB gzip, per CLAUDE.md), the hero's limit is the 1.35–1.6 s animation, not the network. The owner's "1.6 s on the 4G line" claim is consistent with that.

### Gaps
- No production build was measured (the brief says not to start or stop servers beyond the dev one). LCP and transfer for `vite build` are unknown here.
- Scroll jank was not measurable in headless software rendering (frame p95 317 ms is an artefact of no GPU).
- Safari and iOS (svh units, sticky stages, `hidden=until-found` support) were not tested.
