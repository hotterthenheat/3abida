# Slayer terminal UX/UI audit: app shell, Pulse, Settings (plus /community and the old addresses)

Scope covered: the rail (open, folded, phone sheet), the subject/search button, the command palette, keyboard shortcuts, the alerts drawer and /alerts, toasts (from code), the theme controls, the mark/wordmark, the market-state signature and clock, menus, the footer as it sits in the terminal, /pulse, /pulse/board, /home, /live-terminal, /workspace, /watchlist, /tracker, /liquidity, /settings and all ten /settings/:section pages, /community, /auditor-log and /skys-vision.

How it was checked (2026-10-09, app at http://localhost:5300, read-only):
- A route sweep: 14 pages × 4 viewports (1440×900, 1280×720, 1920×1080, 390×844) × 2 themes = 112 screenshots, plus 12 redirects. Each run collected console errors and warnings, page errors, failed requests (status ≥ 400), document and `<main>` horizontal overflow, elements running past the viewport, banned words in the visible text, unlabelled controls, and phone tap targets under 44 px. Script: `scratchpad/audit-shell/sweep.mjs`. Results: `scratchpad/audit-shell/sweep2.json`.
- Interaction probes that click every rail item, fold the tree, fold the rail and reload, Tab through the page, use the palette (Ctrl+K, arrows, Tab, queries, Enter), open and close the alerts drawer (button, Esc, scrim, /alerts, Back), and walk the footer and the 404 pages (`shell.mjs`). A second probe covers every Pulse desk, the add-widget menu, removing and adding panels, Save as, delete, the tile "open page" button and its way back, the board, every Settings section, every Settings button, field validation, persistence after reload, the theme tiles, the clear dialog, invite copy and /community (`pulse.mjs`). A third covers the phone strip, the phone menu, the phone palette, the phone board, Settings on a phone, and the Tab order from page load (`phone.mjs`). A fourth times how long Esc and a scrim click take to close the drawer (`esc.mjs`).
- Source reading of src/App.tsx, src/components/layout/*, src/components/alerts/*, src/pages/workspace/*, src/pages/PulseBoard.tsx, src/pages/settings/*, src/pages/community/Room.tsx and src/components/ui/ComingSoonWall.tsx, checked against .claude/CLAUDE.md.
- Screenshots: `research_notes/Slayer full UX UI audit/shots-shell/`. Files named `<route>__<viewport>__<theme>.png` come from the sweep. `x_*` come from the shell probe, `y_*` from the Pulse/Settings probe and `z_*` from the phone probe.

Severity scale: **Broken** (does not work or reads as broken), **Major** (a real user-facing defect or a breach of the owner's rules), **Minor** (a small defect or friction), **Polish** (finish).

## 1. Does every control do something visible and correct (rail, tabs, menus, toggles, inputs, Settings, redirects, persistence)?

### Takeaway
Navigation, redirects, desk switching, panel add and remove, Save as, the way back from a tile, Settings validation and persistence all work. Settings, though, holds **12 dead buttons** that look live. The command palette's arrow keys **lose the highlight off-screen**. The rail **hides Settings** whenever the window is 720 px tall or a product's page tree is open. Pulse shows **up to four different prices for one name on one screen**.

### Cited Findings

**What works well (verified)**
- Every rail item lands on its product. The section row then folds and unfolds its tree without navigating, and the tree's silver fill tracks the current page — `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-3abida/3f13cba1-d81b-5e53-bbee-8bc889ed9a96/scratchpad/audit-shell/shell.log` (CLICK lines for all 11 items); src/components/layout/SideNav.tsx:388-394.
- The rail's folded state survives a reload. The folded rail shows hover tips ("Terrain") — shell.log "collapsed after reload true", screenshot `x_shell_collapsed.png`.
- Every old address redirects to a live page: /home, /live-terminal and /workspace go to /pulse; /watchlist to /weigher; /tracker and /auditor-log to /compass/tracker; /skys-vision to /compass; /liquidity to /trace/live-tape; /alerts to /pulse with the drawer open — sweep2.json redirect rows; [App.tsx](src/App.tsx) routes.
- Pulse: the four presets switch with a fade. Add widget adds the panel in the first hole. Remove works. Reset preset restores the template. Save as creates a desk and blocks preset names. Delete removes it. The tile button "Open the Map" goes to /pinpoint/map with a "Back to Pulse" pill that works — pulse.log (DESK, after add, ONE PANEL, after reset, saved as Mine, TILE FULL BUTTONS, wayback lines); screenshot `y_wayback.png`.
- Settings: /settings goes to /settings/account. All ten sections render. Email "abc" gives "That is not an email address" and handle "a b" gives "One word, no spaces". A new name reaches the rail at once and survives a reload. Sound switches survive a reload. "Unsubscribe" turns every switch off and the button reads "Done". The clear dialog opens and Esc closes it. Invite "Copy" turns to "Copied." — pulse.log; screenshot `y_settings_field_errors.png`.
- No console errors, no page errors and no failed requests on any of the 124 loads. The only console output is React Router's v7 future-flag warning — sweep2.json (CONSOLE, PAGEERR and FAILED are empty).

### Gaps

## 2. Console errors, overflow, clipping, overlaps, loading states across 1440×900, 1280×720, 1920×1080, 390×844, dark and light

### Takeaway
The code is clean: no console errors, React warnings, AG Grid warnings or failed requests on any of the 124 loads, and no horizontal page scroll at any size. The visual problems are layout ones: the live chart's toolbar overlapping its legend at 1280, charts leaving a third to half of their width empty, clipped axes, the "Reset" pill over a level label, and Settings' three-column plan grid crushed on a phone.

### Cited Findings
- Console, page errors and failed requests: none on any route, theme or viewport. The one warning is React Router's "v7_startTransition" future flag, on every load — sweep2.json (the CONSOLE, PAGEERR and FAILED summaries are empty once Future Flag is excluded).
- Horizontal overflow: `document.scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` and `main.scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` on every load, including 390 px Settings — sweep2.json `WIDE {}` and phone.log `phone settings … {"sw":390,"cw":390,"wide":[]}`.
